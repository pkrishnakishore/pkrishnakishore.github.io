01 / Research agenda

Political consultants and
authority within parties

Proposed doctoral research

When do political consultants shift decision-making authority inside parties, and toward whom?

I propose to examine how party leaders’ control over consultant resources and grassroots cadres’ local knowledge shape the distribution of effective authority. Indian political parties provide a setting for a broader comparative inquiry into party organization, delegation, and representation.

The proposed design uses comparative cases, document analysis, semi-structured interviews, and process tracing of decisions such as candidate selection and campaign resource allocation. A central challenge is distinguishing consultant influence from leaders’ pre-existing efforts to centralize authority.

This is a developing research agenda. It does not present completed fieldwork or findings.
