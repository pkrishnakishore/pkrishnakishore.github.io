01 / Research approach

Connecting questions,
methods, and evidence

Engineering training, policy analysis, and research practice inform how I frame problems and assess explanations.

Engineering & systems

Define the system. Examine the assumptions.

Engineering training and industrial R&D experience inform my approach to measurement, test-data interpretation, validation, and the relationships between parts of a system. Emissions-policy work extended that technical perspective into comparative regulation and implementation.

Policy & institutions

Understand incentives and context.

At Columbia SIPA and in UN research, I examined how institutions, stakeholder relationships, and implementation conditions shape policy. Client projects on agricultural innovation and development partnerships connected technological possibilities with questions of access, coordination, and adoption.

Data & mixed methods

Bring different forms of evidence into the same inquiry.

My research practice combines survey analysis, interviews, qualitative coding, comparative document analysis, and evidence mapping. Computing coursework adds Python, SQL, BigQuery, and dashboard development to this toolkit; the coursework projects are identified separately below.

Evidence synthesis & collaboration

Make findings traceable and usable.

UN evaluation work, ESIC publications, and Cochrane study coordination connect evidence extraction and data quality with research communication. I contribute through synthesis, structured workflows, report drafting, and coordination across people with different forms of expertise.
